## description

Our mission was to remodel and beautify an old loft space in Tribeca. Dealing with a very small elevator and having to carry most of the interior finishes up the stairs was a challenge. We worked with boom lifts to hoist millwork through the window to insure quality. We handled fabrications of custom millwork furniture matching decorative ceiling details and intricate wall coverings. Streamline self performed the millwork and finishes.
