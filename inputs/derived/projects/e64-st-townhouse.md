## description

A 3800 sq foot 90% gut renovation. The clients chose to keep some of the historical features intact for this amazing build out. The door trims, the staircase, and the window moldings are all original and refurbished. All new mechanicals and windows throughout modernize the project while the millwork fits in perfectly with the style of the home. Level 5 finishing throughout and upholstered walls make this house a perfect 10. Streamline was the General Contractor for this project.
