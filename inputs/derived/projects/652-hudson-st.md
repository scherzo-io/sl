## description

Located just steps away from what many consider the center of the universe, the corner of 14th st and Hudson st in the meat packing district has old NY charachter with cobble stone streets and pre war buildings, combined with the new tech buildings such as apple. This Hudson St loft brings decades of charachter back to brand new look and feel. Built-in studios worked hand in hand with the owner and designer to capture every detail in the space.
