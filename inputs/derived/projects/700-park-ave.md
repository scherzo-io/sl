## description

The stunning Park Ave duplex with terrace has been passed down from generation to generation. Designer Irene Pappas captures all the amazing sunlight and accents it with the white background throughout the beautifully laid out floor plan. Custom stairs with brass Julius Bloom balusters and handrail turn the entrance into a masterpiece. Inlays in the tile floor and wall to wall marble slabs in the primary bathroom are book matched to perfection. The Level 5 finishing through and the accents on the terrace make this apartment one of a kind.
