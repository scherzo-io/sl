About us

Eric ortense

Eric is a senior sales and marketing leader who excels at communicating the strengths, vision, and value proposition of the organization to others. As the son of a developer, Eric grew up in the trades and transitioned into management. With a life history in Manhattan, he considers NYC to be his backyard.
Liam treanor

A highly qualified executive with over 20 years of experience managing high level projects. Liam started as a finish carpenter and now manages his staff of nearly 100 team members. His strengths are in full scale renovations, change of use and out of ground projects.

MICHAEL COLLINS

Michael brings 35 years of residential construction experience in New York City to Streamline. Ever mindful of the personal dimensions of residential work, he enjoys interacting with our clients and their designers to achieve their visions for townhouses, lofts, and apartments.
