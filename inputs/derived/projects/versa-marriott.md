## description

Our mission was to build a mega restaurant with retractable roof Versa, in the brand new Marriott Renaissance Hotel. This included coordination of three participating design teams. A custom fabricated retractable roof was lifted in sections with a crane to the rooftop of an adjacent property for assembly before being slid into place. Large critical path items, like the retractable roof, needed to be carefully planned to coordinate with a new high rise building still under construction. Streamline's role for this project was Construction Manager.
