## Don't

- **Don't push.** The remote is public and Alexey hasn't flipped it.
- Don't commit image binaries, video files, the deck PDFs, or anything under `wp-content/`.
- Don't delete `public/photos` or `public/videos/` — both are git-ignored and regenerated by the
  scripts above. If they vanish, slots render an empty dark field and *nothing errors*, which is
  easy to mistake for a bug.
- Don't re-run `build-image-manifest.mjs --uploads` and commit the result casually. The build
  reads that manifest now, so changing it changes the site.
- Don't open, OCR, screenshot or quote the decks' REFERENCES pages (commercial p41, residential
  p42–43).
- Don't edit `PLAN.md` §1 rows. Propose a new one in your session log.
- Don't touch `app/**`, `sanity/**`, `content/copy/**` unless Alexey assigns it.

## Two traps that already cost time

- `next start` holds port 8080 after its shell exits, so you can spend an hour verifying a build
  you didn't make. Kill it first, every time.
- Images are lazy. In a background browser tab `document.hidden` is true, nothing intersects, and
  `complete` stays false forever — the app is fine and your check is wrong. Foreground the tab or
  assert on the markup.
