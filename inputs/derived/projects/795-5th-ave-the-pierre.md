## description

With views across Central Park, this 5th avenue stunner is nothing short of spectacular in both design and architecture. The Pierre Hotel has its residencies at the top floors and this 2 bedroom has super high end features throughout. The metal cladded galley kitchen consists of brass wrapped cabinets, glass and metal suspended shelves and beautiful sequence matched stone. The wall coverings and custom moldings have a French appeal while the floors looking like something out of a story book. The team at Streamline USA took great care in bringing the design teams vision to life. And it shows!
