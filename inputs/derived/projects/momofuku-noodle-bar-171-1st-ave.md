## description

Rebuild and refresh was the mission for this decade old establishment. Every inch of the front of house and back of house was rebuilt up to modern standards and code with all new requirements. Our team had to break down the kitchen, fully demo the tile floor, level the beams and reinstall every inch in record time. Along the way, we sanded and stained all the walls and bar, updated all the electrical and plumbing, and made major improvements with a new HVAC system throughout.
