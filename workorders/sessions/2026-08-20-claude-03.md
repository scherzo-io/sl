## Did

1. **Diagnosed the switcher before touching it**, in the browser. Three separate causes, all
   real: it did not look like a control; the Homepage group is genuinely inert under direction C
   because Archive renders an index and no hero; and picking a video variant produced a blank
   screen because nothing was staged. State persists in `localStorage`, so a reviewer who landed

   merely unclear.

   `content/video-inventory.tsv`, remuxes each file with `-movflags +faststart` (stream copy — no
   re-encode, not one pixel altered) into the git-ignored `public/videos`, and grabs a real
   poster frame at t=4s with ffmpeg.
3. **Built the two variants Alexey asked for**: `video-loop` at full strength and `video-tint`
   under a 45% black. Muted, looped, `playsInline`, held on the poster under
   `prefers-reduced-motion`.
4. **Rebuilt the switcher** as a labelled button plus a 320px panel over the content: grouped
   options with a one-line hint each, radio markers, Esc to close, Reset to defaults, groups
   disabled *with the reason*, and unavailable variants greyed from server-resolved
   capabilities.

## Decisions I made on my own

- **45% for the tint.** Chosen by eye against the red wordmark and the nav at 1440. It is one
  number in `HomeVideo.tsx` and I flagged it for Cursor to second-guess with a screenshot.
- **Stream copy, not transcode.** faststart makes playback start immediately; re-encoding
  1024×576 footage would only lose more. The footage is untouched.
- **Poster at t=4s** — past any fade-up, well before the end card, and a real frame rather than
  black.
- **Disabled the Homepage group under Archive rather than hiding it.** Hiding options makes a
  panel feel unstable; saying "not used by Archive" explains the build instead.
- **Added Reset.** Not asked for, but it is the actual fix for "doesn't work" — persisted state
  is what traps a reviewer on an empty variant.
- **Kept `video-scroll`.** PLAN §5 lists it; it now plays too rather than being a third blank.
