## description

A 5,500 sq ft full gut renovation of an existing 4 story townhouse. We added a penthouse extension to the house, elevator and a passive thermo rating, creating a truly elegant traditional townhouse. Working side by side with very old neighboring properties and requiring seismic monitoring during the process. We worked together with the architects and designers during the rollout of the full design package while taking into account all the details required. Streamline was the General Contractor for this project.
