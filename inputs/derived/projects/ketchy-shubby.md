## description

Bringing lounge and late night restaurant life back to SoHo in a big way, Ketchy Shuby is an amazing new concept by owner operator Jamie Mulholland and his team. This group will deliver a late night dining experience along with a premium lounge experience in the cellar. The concept comes together with the massive tree in the dining room and the roots appearing on the ceiling of the cellar. Streamline USA worked closely with ownership and design team to capture the ultimate good vibes in the build out.
