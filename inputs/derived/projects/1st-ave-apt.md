## description

Our goal was to gut renovate a poorly laid out apartment and modernize it. This project was on the 31st floor- 2 bedroom 2.5 bathroom apartment. Challenges: Working in a high rise building with multiple construction projects going on. Elevator use and logistics for operations is always a challenge. Bringing to life the vision of the design team within the budget of the client. Lessons: Having all the design intent worked out prior to start of the project. Utilizing the budget as a base line in design. Using the amazing views of the city as a back drop always creates a nice working environment. Streamline's role was GC, and we self performed the millwork.
