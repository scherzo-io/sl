## description

Streamline created a custom blackened steel look for a fashion company’s corporate office space. The owner realized his vision of creating a space that combined both new and old elements and styles. In the beginning, we decided to perform all welding onsite which allowed smooth installation of the featured custom steel windows. Keeping deliveries to a minimum enabled us to adhere to the rigorous schedule and finish the job on time and budget. Challenge: Complete work in a building with numerous restrictions and a tight schedule to the move-in date. Streamline's role was GC, and we self performed the millwork.
