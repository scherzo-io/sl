## description

A 7000 sq ft 5 floor townhouse overlooking Washington Sq Park with a stunning IPE Roofdeck and a gorgeous blue stone back yard. This project was a full gut renovation that kept all the integrity of the original house in the brick and moldings that were landmarked. Streamline performed all the carpentry, finish carpentry and assisted in the millwork and finishes throughout.
