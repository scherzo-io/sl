## description

A 7,000 sq ft gut renovation of a part of the old homestead steakhouse on 9th ave in meatpacking district. New staircase and elevator. We had to convert an old Homestead Steakhouse landmark building into a custom retail operation. Working on one of the busiest streets in the city was one of our challenges. Streamline performed the carpentry and finishes for this project.
