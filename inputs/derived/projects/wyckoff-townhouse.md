## description

To gut renovate the garden floor, outfit with all new millwork and refinish all of the upper floors. The floors were sanded throughout so the colors matched on the older and new floor areas. We did a full level 5 paint job which included the stairs, moldings and doors throughout. There was installation of new recessed lighting in all the areas on the main floor as well as relocation of the AV system for the house. This classic century-old Brooklyn townhouse looks brand new!
