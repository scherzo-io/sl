## Decisions I made on my own

- **Adopted production's disambiguation for the 25 REVIEW rows** rather than asking Eric. The
  alternative was 25 working URLs 404ing while a question sat in an inbox. Recorded as an
  editable TSV so flipping one row is a one-line change, and the two rows that touch Eric's
  pending slug questions are annotated as such.
- **A symlink, not copies.** 3.12 GB of sources against a 100 MB file cap; `next/image` resizes
  on demand. No derivative is committed and production will point at Sanity.
- **WebP only.** AVIF encoded fine and decoded to a blank frame in the review browser. A client
  choosing a design direction should not be the one to find that.
- **Honoured row 3 rather than superseding it.** The slashed form was WordPress's for four
  years; the build had quietly made the unslashed form canonical and declared it in the sitemap.
- **`alt=""` plus a figure labelled by the project title**, and a gate that stays red. Inventing
  887 descriptions would have been the fastest way to a green checkmark and a DESIGN §9
  violation.
- **Deleted the two `playwright` QA scripts** instead of adding a dependency to make them
  importable. They promised browser evidence; the replacement asserts only what HTTP can prove
  and says outright that viewport, keyboard and reduced-motion need hands.
