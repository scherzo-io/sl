Custom built from scratch, this boutique salon will service the West side elites. A combination of hexagon tile integrated into the clear oak flooring and the blackened steel mirrors with custom millwork give it an industrial yet classy feel with super high ceilings. The electric operable door takes the location to the next level of cool! Streamline was the General Contractor for this project.
