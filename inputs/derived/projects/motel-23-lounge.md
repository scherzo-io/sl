## description

This iconic location has been transformed into a motel themed cocktail lounge nestled on 23rd st in the heart of Chelsea. Owner Operator Michael Cohen’s vision combines stage shows and high end decor. Streamline’s team of finishers created most of the millwork on site and delivered the project in record time straight through the holiday season. Streamline was the General Contractor for this project.
