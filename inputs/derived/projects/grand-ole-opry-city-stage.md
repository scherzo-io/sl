## description

Our mission was to create a city stage sister venue to the biggest named country music stage in the world located in Nashville. Challenges: Bringing to life the same excitement in a new state of the art technical venue. To work on Broadway in Times Square with logistical issues that are physically unmatched. Manning the job with hundreds of men to run at a fast paced schedule. Working in conjunction with teams of specialists in another state, while coordinating activity on the site daily. Streamline's role for this project was Construction Manager.
