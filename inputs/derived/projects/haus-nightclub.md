## description

Located at the corner of Canal St and West Broadway, this location is in the prime zone of NYC hospitality. Canal Street has come a very long way over the years and Haus nightclub has helped take it into the direction of up. This 5500 sq foot space boasts stadium seating, a custom LED chandelier and tiger eye stone bar top. Ownership spares no expense to bring the best display of finishes they could to the customers. The front doors are part o the show. Streamline's role was GC, and we self performed the millwork and finishes.
